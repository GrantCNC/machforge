## Typical Manufacturing Scope

- **Processes:** CNC milling (3-, 4-, and 5-axis as appropriate), CNC turning, laser cutting, and laser marking
- **Order stage:** prototypes from 1 piece and low-volume production; MOQ 1 piece
- **Materials:** 6061 / 7075 aluminum, 304 / 316L stainless steel, brass, copper, engineering plastics, and titanium or other materials on request
- **Finishing:** Type II / Type III anodizing, PVD coating, sandblasting, and electropolishing
- **Engineering and production support:** DFM review, partner-manufacturer coordination, production follow-up, inspection coordination, and quality documentation; CMM inspection where required

## Typical Project Details

- **Initial DFM feedback:** typically 24–48 hours, depending on project complexity and the information provided
- **Prototype lead time:** typically 3–7 business days after requirements are confirmed
- **Low-volume production lead time:** typically 10–15 business days, depending on quantity, complexity, finishing, and inspection scope
- **Tolerance reference:** standard CNC features can often be produced around ±0.05 mm; ±0.01 mm may be achievable on selected features after process review. These are not blanket tolerances for every dimension.
- **Confidentiality:** NDA available

Lead times, tolerances, and inspection scope depend on the specific part and confirmed requirements. Production and shipping times are quoted separately.

## How MachForge Works

MachForge acts as the engineering and production-coordination point. Parts are manufactured by partner manufacturers selected for each project's geometry, material, tolerances, quantity, and finishing requirements; MachForge coordinates the technical discussion, production follow-up, inspection requirements, and delivery.

MachForge is based in Shenzhen, China, and primarily supports hardware teams in North America and Europe. Projects from other international markets may be supported depending on project fit.
